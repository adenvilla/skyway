    What moves the price

    Four things decide your number.

    Know these before you ask for a quote and you'll spot a fair price from a bad one.

    Chargeable weight

    Air rates use the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (length × width × height ÷ 6,000). Light, bulky cargo pays on volume.

    Mode and lane

    Air is faster and dearer per kilo; sea is cheaper per cubic metre but takes weeks. Distance and port congestion do the rest.

    Duty and VAT

    Kenyan duty depends on the tariff code, not the freight rate. We itemise duty and VAT separately and give you the HS code in writing.

    Delivery and handling

    Port charges, transport to your door, and any storage or demurrage. All of it appears on your invoice — nothing added at the end.

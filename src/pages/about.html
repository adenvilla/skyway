Our story

Built on the Eastleigh trading floor.

Skyway began where most Kenyan importers begin: in Eastleigh, among traders bringing goods in from Guangzhou. What was missing was a forwarder who would answer the phone after the container landed — so we became one.

Today we clear and forward cargo for traders, families, NGOs and manufacturers across the country. In 2023 we introduced buy-now-pay-later shipping, letting small importers settle freight in instalments as their stock sells.

How we work

Three things we don't do.

Read any freight complaint online and it usually comes down to one of these.

We don't hide charges

Freight, duty, port fees and delivery are itemised. If a charge appears after your quote, we explain what changed and why — before you pay it.

We don't disappear at the port

Clearing is done in-house by our own licensed agents. Nobody hands your file to a third party the moment it gets difficult.

We don't sell you air for sea cargo

If sea will do, we say so — even though air pays us more per kilo. The right mode for your goods is the one we quote.

What we do

Four disciplines,
one continuous chain.

Most importers deal with a forwarder, a clearing agent, a transporter and a warehouse. Skyway is all four — which is why your cargo moves without being handed around.

  1. 01

    Air freight

    Thrice-weekly departures from China and Dubai into JKIA, 4–7 days to our Nairobi and Mombasa warehouses.

  2. 02

    Sea cargo

    LCL and full container loads through Mombasa on Maersk, PIL and COSCO sailings — including oversized cargo.

  3. 03

    Clearing & documentation

    IDF, entry lodgement, duty assessment and port release handled in-house, then delivered to your doorstep.

  4. 04

    Trucking & warehousing

    Our own fleet from half-tonne pick-ups to 40-tonne semi-trailers, plus secure storage in Nairobi and Mombasa.

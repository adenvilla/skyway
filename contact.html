Questions

Asked before every first shipment.

How do I start a shipment?

Send the supplier details, cargo description, weight or volume, and destination. We reply with a quote and the documents we need. If you already have goods with a supplier, ask them to hold until we confirm collection.

Do you collect from the supplier?

Yes, in Guangzhou, Foshan, London, Manchester, Istanbul, Bursa, Dubai and across New Jersey and Houston. Collection outside those areas is arranged case by case.

What documents do I need?

A commercial invoice and packing list, your KRA PIN, and an IDF. For restricted goods we may ask for permits or certificates. We prepare the entry and tell you exactly what's missing.

Is duty included in your quote?

Freight is quoted separately from duty, VAT and port charges so you can see what the government takes and what we charge. We can pay duty on your behalf where agreed.

Can I pay in instalments?

On approved accounts, yes. We settle your supplier or freight invoice at origin and you clear the balance as your stock sells. Terms depend on the cargo and your trading history.

Do you deliver upcountry?

Yes — Eldoret, Kisumu, Nakuru, Machakos, Meru and beyond, using our own fleet or vetted partners. Ask for the destination rate when you request a quote.
